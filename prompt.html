The RTC-C Prompting Blueprint

Generative AI draws from vast datasets. Giving it clear boundaries acts like sending a job description to a highly capable intern.

R Persona

Role

Who should the AI act as? Defining an expert identity directs vocabulary, depth, and tone.

"Act as an encouraging peer writing tutor..."
Why it works: Limits AI's search space to specialized domain expertise.
T Action

Task

What explicit action must the AI perform? Use strong, specific action verbs instead of generic queries.

"Brainstorm 3 thesis options & critique main arguments..."
Why it works: Direct action verbs eliminate fluff and unfocused output.
C Background

Context

What background details apply? Include your course name, skill level, or personal constraints.

"I am an FYE student working 25 hrs/week in retail..."
Why it works: Personalizes answers to your exact scenario instead of generic advice.
C Guardrails

Constraints

What boundaries, rules, or formatting apply? Define what to do AND what NOT to do.

"Do not write the essay. Limit to a bulleted table under 150 words."
Why it works: Protects academic integrity and guarantees concise formatting.

Why does RTC-C dramatically improve AI answers?

Large Language Models (LLMs) operate on probabilistic matching. When you input a single vague word like "essay help", the model calculates standard responses based on millions of general internet posts. By layering Role, Task, Context, and Constraints, you narrow the statistical target space directly to your specific course requirements and personal learning goals.

Critical AI Literacy & Ethics Checklist

AI tools are pattern recognizers—not truth engines. Use this audit before submitting academic work.

The Student Pre-Submission Audit

Spotting Hallucinations

AI models predict words based on statistical probability, not actual comprehension. They often fabricate authentic-sounding paper titles or citations. Rule of thumb: Never paste a quote or statistic into your homework without checking your college library database.

Detecting Algorithmic Bias

Training data reflects historical internet biases. Follow up with counter-prompts like: "What alternative cultural perspectives or critical viewpoints are missing from this analysis?"

Syllabus Disclosure Statement

Include a brief statement at the end of your assignment when using AI for brainstorming:

"Acknowledgement: I utilized Microsoft Copilot to assist in brainstorming thesis options and organizing my essay outline. All final drafting represents my original work."
